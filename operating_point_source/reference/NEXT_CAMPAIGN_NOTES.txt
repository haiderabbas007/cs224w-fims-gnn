FIMS next-campaign settings record — 12 November 2026
DESIGN ONLY. No detector change, simulation, readout replay or hit-model fit has
been performed by this reporting package. NEXT_CAMPAIGN_SETTINGS.json records
the source evidence, proposed choices and pilot checks in machine-readable form.

Peter's reply removes the need to wait for a finalized detector specification.
For this proof of principle he accepts E=1 kV/cm, B=1 T, drift speed=1 cm/us,
transverse diffusion=300 um/sqrt(cm), and longitudinal diffusion=200 um/sqrt(cm).
This approval is not a measured gas model or Tanner's numerical calculation.

The pinned old readout already uses 0.01 mm/ns, which is 1 cm/us, and exactly
those diffusion coefficients. The earlier conversational value 0.1 cm/us was
incorrect for this code. Existing arrival times do not need changing merely
to match the newly approved speed. Diffusion widths grow with sqrt(distance).

Other changes ARE substantive. Captured Geant4 source has B=1.5 T along +z,
a radius 50 mm, length 100 mm gas cylinder, and a gun distributed over the full
gas volume. Charge drifts to the nearer z=-50/+50 mm endcap. There is no
explicit electric force field: drift is imposed by the ideal readout. Its
generic grid is 5x5 chips per endcap, 20 mm per chip, 256x256 pixels per chip,
giving 78.125 um pitch. It is not the earlier illustrative 50 um layout.

For the next version, a concrete minimal proposal is to retain that cylinder
and pixel grid, use a central gun at (0,0,0), change B to 1 T, and collect charge
at z=+50 mm only. This is equivalent to a centre at z=50 mm in coordinates
from 0 to 100 mm. Maximum drift becomes 100 mm/10 us instead of 50 mm/5 us.
Electron drift toward +z corresponds to E toward -z. These coordinate/layout
choices are proposals under Peter's guidance, not additional specifications
he supplied. No 500 mm chamber or 50 um pixel change is being assumed.

Before generating a new sample, the code must explicitly distinguish a nominal
readout field from an electric force applied to Geant4 trajectories. Merely
adding E=1 kV/cm to metadata would not implement that force. An electromagnetic
field requires the appropriate equation/integration and tests of energy change
and field boundaries; the existing magnetic-only configuration is insufficient.
The approximate Ar/CO2 gas material uses 80/20 mass fractions, not a validated
volume-mixture transport model. Preserve and state that approximation.

The immediate reporting job uses saved predictions on the OLD development
sample. It explores 90%, 95%, 99% primary-charge retention and score/event-loss
distributions without retraining. These targets are not safety requirements.

For the arrival pilot, existing exported unquantized arrival times were obtained
by exact replay of the original readout, not by jittering 25 ns bins. Keep the
original site branch and match events/splits/labels. A clocked arrival model gets
x, y, 25 ns timestamp and arrival order, not true depth or event labels. Separate
arrivals sharing a clock tick remain separate observations. If two exact
simulated times tie, score both before either updates state; never present an
arbitrary file-row order as a measured acquisition sequence. Exact-time inputs
belong to a separately labeled ideal reference.

Freeze a truth-independent stream schedule; no reset is allowed just because
the simulation event ID changes. A nonoverlapping first pilot is useful but
does not demonstrate pileup robustness. Test future-data perturbations, hidden
boundary changes, tie permutations, and chunked-state replay. History must be
defined in time, not number of hits. For old two-endcap data, preserve readout
plane separation. Existing site models see a complete current bin after it
closes, so their latency differs from immediate-arrival decisions.

Validate unit conversions, drift endpoints, diffusion variance, source/field
identity, charge/acceptance accounting and label alignment before a small fixed
pilot. Keep empty events. Preserve all historical results under their original
version. A later muon/pion dE/dx comparison needs new species samples; the
electron-only dataset cannot establish particle separation. This design record does not launch mass production.
